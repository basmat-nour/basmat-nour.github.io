Orphans World Cup 2027

Champions of Will.. to Warsaw

Together for change: our journey to the World Cup of Children from Care Homes. Basmat Nour organizes and coordinates Egypt's delegation to the 11th "Hope for Mundial" tournament, at the invitation of the organizer in Poland, in late May or early June 2027.

Poland

Egypt's team at the World Cup for children from care homes

Basmat Nour coordinates Egypt's delegation to the 11th Hope for Mundial tournament in Warsaw, Poland, in late May or early June 2027. Egypt is represented by the team "Egypt's Orphans National Team".

This is not only a football trip. It is a journey of building a person.

The opening whistle in Warsaw comes after months of work. We do not send boys to play a match and come back. We prepare a generation that knows its worth, stands before the world with its head held high, and carries Egypt's flag with the confidence of those who know who they are and where they are going. That is why every player goes through a full preparation program before travelling, following the approach of the "Ehna Menkom" qualification program.

01

Psychological and community preparation

Psychologists build each player's self-confidence and sense of belonging to his country, prepare him for pressure, travel and contact with new cultures, and hold storytelling sessions with graduates who walked the road before him, so he comes back stronger than he left.

02

Preparation camps

Intensive physical and technical training, twice a week on the Wadi Degla Club pitches for six months, accompanied by camps that build discipline, team spirit and readiness.

03

Character building

Commitment, leadership, sportsmanship and responsibility, because the real championship is for the boy to move from the receiver's seat to the leader's seat, on the pitch and off it.

The invitation

Egypt at the World Cup

The "Hope for Mundial" organization in Warsaw invited Egypt's team to represent the country at the 11th World Cup of Children from Care Homes, and confirmed that the team is organized and coordinated by Basmat Nour Association. The tournament takes place in Warsaw in late May or early June 2027. Its purpose is both sport and integration.

Egypt's team has been a guest of the organizer at previous tournaments and left a good impression. The Egyptian participation matters to the organizers, both organizationally and as part of the tournament.

What the organizer covers

Hotel accommodation for the team for three nights, board, assistance from volunteers, medical care, interpreter services, and transfers from the airport to the hotel and from the hotel to the stadium and events. Travel costs and any additional stay are covered by the participating team.

A pioneering development and sports program to prepare and train Egypt's first delegation to represent the country in the 11th World Cup of Children from Care Homes. It is a full training program that turns sport into a way of building character and creating inspiring ambassadors for the nation.
