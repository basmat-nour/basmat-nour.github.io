Ehna Menkom Initiative

Ehna Menkom: "We Are From You"

Sharing experience and social integration: a program that prepares youth in care homes for independence, led by care-home graduates themselves.

14 - 18years old, the target group
2implementation phases
4field program tracks
3outputs and sustainability measures
Initiative profile

A full preparation program before leaving care

The initiative offers a professional, life-skills, psychological and sports program for young people before they leave care. It relies on direct contact with successful care-home graduates, under the supervision of specialists.

01

Initiative name

Ehna Menkom: sharing experience and social integration.

02

Supervising and implementing body

Basmat Nour Association for Bridges of Development.

03

Target group

Boys and girls in orphan care homes, aged 14 to 18, preparing for independence.

04

General goal

A professional, life-skills, psychological and sports program before they enter society.

Implementation structure

Two connected phases

1

Preparing the leaders (ambassadors)

One to one and a half months, with a specialized partner company of Basmat Nour. A selected group of successful care-home graduates is trained in community leadership, communication, sound psychological foundations and storytelling, so they become the initiative's leaders and share their experience with children professionally.

2

Field implementation inside the homes

Work teams, made up of the graduate leaders and Basmat Nour specialists, visit care homes to deliver the full Ehna Menkom program to young people aged 14 to 18.

Opening day

An official launch and a first pilot

Opening day is the official launch of the initiative, the graduation of the first phase, and the first pilot of the second phase.

01

The ceremony

Announcing the official start of the initiative, graduating the first cohort, honoring the successful graduates who completed the training, and handing them their roles as field leaders.

02

The hands-on session

Hosting children and youth from more than one care home. The leaders deliver the program in practice for the first time through storytelling circles and interactive stations, while Basmat Nour specialists and scouts run an initial psychological, behavioral and sports screening.

The field program

Four parallel tracks inside care homes

01

Life skills

Storytelling circles led by the initiative leaders, sharing real experiences of facing society, with open questions. Practical know-how on managing money, budgeting, understanding rental contracts, securing safe housing and building healthy relationships.

02

Career

Simple workshops to choose an educational or vocational path, hands-on training in writing a CV and handling interviews with confidence, and working inside a workplace.

03

Psychological support and behavior

Basmat Nour psychologists observe issues such as fear of the future, aggression and social phobia, run group awareness sessions, and refer individual cases to behavior programs.

04

Sports

Sports activities that break down psychological barriers, talent discovery by scouts and coaches, and placing talents in Basmat Nour's sports teams to support them and open a professional path.

Outputs and sustainability

The impact continues after the visits end

01

Mentorship network

Each young person in the home is linked to a successful graduate, with a life coach alongside, who follows up by phone and in person to give continuous support and help solve everyday problems.

02

Extended support

The association keeps caring for behavioral and psychological cases and sports talents after the program period in the home ends.

03

Impact report

Periodic reports showing progress in the youth's awareness and the success of integration.
